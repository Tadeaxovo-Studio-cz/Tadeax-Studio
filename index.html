About us

Ahoj! We are Tadeax Studio

Tadeax Studio is an independent creative studio from Czechia. It started as a YouTube channel and grew into a place where we build websites, visual identities and playful digital worlds.

Behind the studio is a YouTuber and programmer with a big passion for trains and public transport. That love shows up in our projects, and we also make music. Today we offer web services, Roblox scripts, thumbnails, logos and music projects.

Everything is made with enthusiasm and a drive to push quality further, even from a simple setup. Our channel has passed 800 subscribers and keeps growing.
